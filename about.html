About Me

Honestly, I’m not writing about a journey with a clearly defined destination. There are many things I’ve started over the years but never finished, and many goals I haven’t reached yet. I’m still figuring things out, and I don’t know exactly where this path will lead me. But I want to document it anyway — the things I learn, the things I build, the challenges I face, and the progress I make. This time, I want to keep going, finish what I start, and hopefully reach the places I’ve been trying to get to for so long.

My Career Journey

My interest in programming started long before I seriously began pursuing it as a career. I remember being interested in programming when I was working at Tipax, and maybe even before that. Over the years, I started learning programming several times, from Python to different online courses, but I never managed to finish them or truly follow through. Even now, I’m still at the beginning of this journey. I’ve started again, this time with Front-End Development, but I don’t know yet where it will take me or whether I’ll reach the place I’m hoping for. What I do know is that I want to give myself another chance to keep going, finish what I start, and finally see what happens if I don’t give up halfway.

My Education Journey

My education journey has never been a particularly ambitious or straightforward one. I was never really a student who studied hard. At the same time, I was never a weak student either. I was somewhere in the middle. During school, I mostly just went through the usual routine without really challenging myself. I never took part in entrance exams for gifted schools, olympiads, or anything like that. I simply studied enough to get through each stage and moved on. Things became a little less predictable during high school. I studied mathematics and physics, but during pre-university, I suddenly decided to take the experimental sciences entrance exam instead. I eventually got a diploma in experimental sciences and spent the next three years preparing for the university entrance exam. After that, I studied Cellular and Molecular Biology at Payame Noor University. Looking back, my education has been full of changes, detours, and decisions that I didn’t always have a clear plan for. And this year, I took the master's entrance exam with the intention of changing my academic path and choosing something closer to the direction I want to take my life and career. I still don't know exactly where this new path will lead me, but for the first time, I feel like I'm making this choice more consciously.

My Development Journey

I officially started learning programming this year by enrolling in Quera’s Front-End Development Bootcamp. However, because of my work and other responsibilities, I fell behind the bootcamp schedule. Instead of giving up, I decided to catch up on my own and continue learning at my own pace. I’m still at the beginning of my development journey, learning the fundamentals and working on projects along the way. I don’t know exactly where this path will take me yet, but for now, I’m focused on learning, building, and most importantly, continuing.

What I'm Learning

I’m currently learning Front-End Development and focusing on building a strong foundation in web development. I started with HTML and CSS, and I’m gradually continuing with Tailwind CSS and JavaScript. As I progress, I also plan to learn React and build more complete and interactive projects. Right now, I’m still learning the fundamentals, practicing what I learn, and trying to understand how everything works together. I’m also working on personal projects because I believe building is one of the best ways to learn. There is still a long way ahead of me, but I’m taking it one step at a time and focusing on making consistent progress.
